A bounded scroll container that clips its content along one declared axis
and replaces platform scrollbar chrome with a token-resolved indicator —
chat panes, log blocks, tag rows, tall menus inside cards. Its size comes
entirely from your layout; scrolling stays native (wheel, touch, keyboard,
indicator drag).

## Accessibility

`label` is required — it names the scroll region for assistive technology.
The scroller scrolls its declared axis only and clips the other; cross-axis
overflow is an authoring mistake (wrap or size content on the cross axis).
Page-level scrolling remains the browser's job.
