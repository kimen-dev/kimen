A short, transient message about something the person just did, shown
without blocking the page. It leaves on its own after its time, which stops
while the person points at the toaster, works inside it or is away from the
page. Raise it into a [`ki-toaster`](../toaster/).

<Demo
  layout="block"
  code={`<ki-toast tone="success" heading="Changes saved" duration="0">
  Workspace settings are up to date.
</ki-toast>`}
/>

## Examples

### Tones

`tone` picks the icon and its ink, and the live-region urgency; the surface
stays the same.

<Demo
  layout="block"
  code={`<ki-toast tone="info" duration="0">Sync complete.</ki-toast>
<ki-toast tone="warning" duration="0">Storage is almost full.</ki-toast>
<ki-toast tone="danger" heading="Upload failed">The file is larger than 20 MB.</ki-toast>`}
/>

### Action

One ghost `ki-button size="sm"`: its click runs, then the toast leaves. A
toast with an action never leaves on its own.

<Demo
  layout="block"
  code={`<ki-toast heading="Message archived">
  The conversation moved to your archive.
  <ki-button slot="action" variant="ghost" size="sm">Undo</ki-button>
</ki-toast>`}
/>

## Accessibility

`danger` toasts expose `role="alert"`, every other tone `role="status"`; no
toast moves focus. Only the heading and the message are announced. The time
stops while the pointer rests on the toaster, while focus is inside it or
while the page is hidden (WCAG 2.2.1); Escape dismisses a focused toast.
`dismiss-label` is the only built-in word: localize it whenever the document
language is not English.
