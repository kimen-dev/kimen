The one place toasts appear: it paints its [`ki-toast`](../toast/) children
in the top layer at a corner of the viewport, stacks them from the top down
in arrival order, shows the newest `max` (3 by default) and queues the older
ones, without covering the page beside them.

```html
<ki-toaster placement="bottom-end"></ki-toaster>
<script>
  document.querySelector('ki-toaster').show({
    tone: 'success',
    title: 'Changes saved',
    message: 'Workspace settings are up to date.',
  });
</script>
```

## Placement

`placement` accepts `top-center`, `top-end`, `bottom-center` and
`bottom-end` (default); `end` follows the writing direction, and a narrow
touch screen always uses the bottom center. The distance from the edges and
the gap between toasts are the `--ki-toaster-*` tokens.

## Accessibility

While it shows toasts the toaster is a group named by `label`
(`Notifications` by default), not a landmark: keep it inside one of the
page's landmarks. F6 moves focus into it and Escape on a toast returns focus
to where it was; an empty toaster exposes nothing.
