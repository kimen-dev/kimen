A token-styled, non-interactive progress indicator for known or unknown
duration work — uploads, downloads, installations, multi-step processing.

<Demo
  layout="block"
  code={`<ki-progress value="64" label="Uploading report.pdf"></ki-progress>`}
/>

## Examples

### Determinate and indeterminate

Use `value`/`max` when the completed fraction is known; use `indeterminate`
when work is ongoing but cannot be measured — including loading-indicator
use cases:

<Demo
  layout="block"
  code={`<ki-progress value="30" max="120" label="Processing 30 of 120 records"></ki-progress>
<ki-progress indeterminate label="Connecting"></ki-progress>`}
/>

### Shapes

`linear` fits page flows and lists; `circular` fits compact or centered
placements:

<Demo
  code={`<ki-progress shape="circular" value="64" label="Uploading report.pdf"></ki-progress>
<ki-progress shape="circular" indeterminate label="Loading results"></ki-progress>`}
/>

## Accessibility

Always set `label` to *what is progressing* ("Uploading report.pdf", never
just "Progress"). For static measurements within a known range (disk usage,
scores) a gauge/meter is the right pattern, and step-by-step wizard
navigation belongs to a future stepper — not this component.
