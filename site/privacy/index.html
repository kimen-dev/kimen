Privacy

This site is documentation for an open-source project. It has no accounts, no login and no advertising, and it never asks for personal data.

What is measured

Page views are counted with Umami, self-hosted by the maintainers at umami.onmars.tech. Umami sets no cookies and builds no cross-site profile; it records the page visited, the referrer, and technical details derived from the request — browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language, and location down to country, region and city — without storing the IP address. Because nothing is stored on your device and no profile is built, no consent banner is required.

What is not done

No advertising networks, no third-party trackers, no session recording, no fingerprinting, and no data sold or shared.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes requests to deliver and protect the site, as any content delivery network does.
