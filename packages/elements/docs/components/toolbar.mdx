# Working with page actions

Place a floating toolbar where the actions are used. Tabs and arrow keys
serve different purposes: Tab moves into or past the group, while arrows
move between its actions. Home and End reach the first and last action.

```html
<ki-toolbar label="Image actions" orientation="vertical">
  <ki-button variant="ghost">Crop</ki-button>
  <ki-divider></ki-divider>
  <ki-button variant="ghost">Rotate</ki-button>
</ki-toolbar>
```

Use a named `ki-icon-button` for each action represented by an icon. With
ToggleIconButton, use its `toggle` and `selected` props for a pressed
action such as Bold. A floating toolbar can pair with a `ki-fab` in the
`fab` slot. The companion remains a separate stop in the Tab sequence.

Use a docked toolbar on compact screens that can dedicate their bottom edge to
page actions. Give the content room to scroll clear of the bar and its safe-area
padding. Opt into `hide-on-scroll` where actions should recede on downward
page scroll and reappear on upward scroll or focus. Choose MobileNav on screens
where the bottom edge is needed for navigation.
