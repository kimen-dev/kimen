Use `ki-reasoning` for supporting steps that a reader can open on demand. Keep
the assistant's answer outside the disclosure. The disclosure starts collapsed
and its summary reads “Thinking…” while the assistant works.

## Thinking

Append steps to the default slot as they arrive. An open disclosure shows the new
steps in order. Set `open` to display the steps immediately.

<Demo code={`<ki-reasoning open>
  <ol>
    <li>Check the inputs.</li>
    <li>Compare the available outcomes.</li>
  </ol>
</ki-reasoning>`} />

## Completed

Set `status="done"` when the assistant finishes. Supply elapsed milliseconds in
`duration` for a summary such as “Thought for 12 seconds.” The component rounds
the elapsed time to the nearest second. Without a valid elapsed time, the
summary reads “Thought.” The disclosure keeps its current open state.

<Demo code={`<ki-reasoning status="done" duration="12000">
  <ol>
    <li>Check the inputs.</li>
    <li>Compare the available outcomes.</li>
  </ol>
</ki-reasoning>`} />

## Accessibility and customization

The native summary opens and closes with Enter or Space. Status changes have a
polite announcement, and reduced motion keeps the thinking text still. Localize
the summary with `thinking-label`, `done-label`, `singular-duration-label`, and
`duration-label`. Include `{seconds}` in the two duration labels where the
number belongs.

Use `--ki-reasoning-fg` to customize the text and `--ki-reasoning-border` to
customize the rule beside the steps. The backing is transparent by default.
Use `--ki-reasoning-bg`, `--ki-reasoning-bg-image`, and
`--ki-reasoning-backdrop-filter` to style it. The `summary` and `body` parts
allow additional styling.

Listen for `ki-toggle` to read `event.detail.open` after a reader opens or closes
the disclosure.
