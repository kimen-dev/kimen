Place a drawer at `start` or `end` to edit details beside a list. Use
`bottom` for a short action sheet. The built-in Close button is available
for every placement. A bottom sheet also closes on a downward swipe from
its handle.

<Demo code={`<ki-button type="button" onclick="this.nextElementSibling.show()">Edit incident</ki-button>
<ki-drawer heading="Edit incident">
  <ki-input label="Title" value="Search index rebuild"></ki-input>
  <ki-button slot="footer" variant="primary" type="button" onclick="this.closest('ki-drawer').close()">Done</ki-button>
</ki-drawer>`} />

Provide a heading to name the drawer. When a modal drawer closes, focus
returns to the previously focused trigger. Use `modal="false"` to keep the
rest of the page interactive, and `close-label` to translate the built-in
button.

The app can veto dismissal by calling `preventDefault()` on `ki-close`.
Wire slotted actions to your app and call `close()` when their work is done.
The `size` property overrides the themed 18rem side width or bottom height
cap.
