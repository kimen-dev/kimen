Use `ki-sources` to show the evidence behind an answer. Give every direct
`ki-source` a unique identifier, destination and title. Place a `ki-citation`
immediately after the supported claim and set its `ref` to that identifier.

<Demo code={`<p>The study design<ki-citation ref="methods"></ki-citation> explains the results.</p>
<ki-sources>
  <ki-source id="methods" href="https://example.org/methods" title="Study design" snippet="How the study ran."></ki-source>
</ki-sources>`} />

The disclosure starts closed. Add `open` to expand it initially. The first
mention decides each number. Repeated mentions reuse that number. Sources
without citations follow in declaration order.

Hover or focus a citation to preview its title and hostname. Escape
dismisses the preview. Activating the citation expands the disclosure
and focuses its source link. The links in the ordered list have their
source titles as names.

Use `label-template`, `source-count-label`, and `sources-count-label`
to localize the labels. Set `source-id` instead of `id` in a guarded spec.
Use the same reference in the citation.
