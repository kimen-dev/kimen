Use a toolbar for a few related page actions. A floating toolbar is a pill
that you place in your layout or over your content. Set
`orientation="vertical"` when the actions run down the side of the content.

<Demo code={`<ki-toolbar label="Formatting">
  <ki-button variant="ghost" size="sm">Bold</ki-button>
  <ki-divider orientation="vertical"></ki-divider>
  <ki-button variant="ghost" size="sm">Insert link</ki-button>
</ki-toolbar>`} />

For a separate primary action, put a companion FAB in `slot="fab"`. It
appears after the floating pill in reading order and has its own Tab stop.
A docked toolbar uses the full bottom edge and includes the device's bottom
safe area. Reserve that edge for its horizontal action group on compact
screens and leave enough space below content for the bar. Use MobileNav
on screens that need navigation at that edge.

Supply a toolbar label, slot actions directly, and give every icon-only
action a name. Tab enters on the most recently focused action. Arrow keys
follow the layout and wrap at the ends. Home and End reach the first and last
available action. Buttons own their activation and toggle state.

Use `hide-on-scroll` to free space while scrolling down the page. Scrolling
up or tabbing to an action brings the surface back. The toolbar stays visible
while an action or the companion FAB has focus.
