Use a carousel for related media, feature highlights or onboarding. Give the
region a short, meaningful label and write each slide as a `ki-carousel-item`.

## Layout and movement

- `single` shows one item by default. Set `slides-per-view` to show multiple
  full items and `slides-per-move` to advance in steps. The final position
  keeps a full view.
- `multi-browse` shows one large item with medium and small previews.
- `hero` shows one large item with a small preview.
- `uncontained` reveals the next item at the edge of the viewport.
- `full-screen` snaps vertically. Set `--ki-carousel-full-screen-block-size`
  to the desired feed height.

For reduced-size previews, give the item decorative media in the `preview`
slot and keep any links and text in its default slot.

Call `next()`, `previous()`, or `goTo(index)`, or set `current`. The index
starts at 1 and reports the leading item. Listen for `ki-slide-change` to
receive the new index in `event.detail.index`.

## Accessibility

The viewport accepts arrow keys, Home and End. Previous and Next also work
with Space and Enter. Slide content stays available while fully visible.
Partially shown previews are inert. Manual changes announce the new position.

Rotation starts only with a positive `autoplay` interval in milliseconds.
Hover suspends rotation. Focus stops it until the reader presses Play.
Reduced motion keeps rotation off and slide movement immediate. Localize the
control and slide labels with the props in the API reference.
