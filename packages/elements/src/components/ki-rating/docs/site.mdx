Use `readonly` to show an average. The visible number keeps its precision and the artwork rounds to the nearest half star.

<Demo code={`<ki-rating readonly label="Average review score" value="4.3"></ki-rating>`} />

Give an editable rating a question in `label`. Tab enters the group, arrows move and select, and Space selects the focused option. Choose `precision="0.5"` to make both halves of each star selectable.

<Demo code={`<ki-rating label="How useful was this answer?" precision="0.5" value="2.5"></ki-rating>`} />

Use `name` to include a selected score in form data and `required` to require an answer. Listen for `ki-change` and read the numeric score from `event.detail.value`.
