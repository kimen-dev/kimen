Use a disclosure for a short explanation, an FAQ answer, a settings section
or tool output. Supply a plain `summary` or slot a heading into the summary
when the disclosure titles a section.

<Demo
  layout="block"
  code={`<ki-details open>
  <h2 slot="summary">Workspace settings</h2>
  <p>Choose who can invite members to the workspace.</p>
</ki-details>`}
/>

The browser manages expansion and can reveal a match in a closed panel.
Press Enter or Space on the focused summary to toggle it. When `disabled`
is set, the summary skips the tab order and open body content stays usable.
Listen to `ki-toggle` for browser-originated state changes and read
`event.detail.open`. Direct writes to `open` are silent on that disclosure.

Use [ki-accordion](../accordion/) for related disclosures. To share exclusivity
without an accordion, give the disclosures the same nonempty `name` in one
document or shadow root. For HTML served
without JavaScript, use native `<details><summary>…</summary>…</details>`
markup.
