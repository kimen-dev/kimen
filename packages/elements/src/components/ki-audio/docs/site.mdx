Use `ki-audio` for voice notes, generated speech and other recordings a
listener chooses to play. Give each recording a concise `title` and link to
its transcript with `transcript-href`.

## Playback and keyboard controls

Playback starts when the listener activates Play. Once the duration is available,
focus Seek and press Left or Right to move five seconds along the timeline.
In a right-to-left layout, Left moves towards the end of the recording.
Press Space on Seek to toggle playback. Focus Playback speed and choose a
rate using the platform's standard select controls.

The title names the player group. The seek control announces the elapsed and
total times, for example “1:24 of 12:05”. Set `time-label` with the
`{elapsed}` and `{duration}` placeholders to translate that announcement.

## Sources and playback events

`src` and `transcript-href` accept relative references and HTTP(S) URLs.
The guarded catalog also applies the host's origin allowlist when configured.
Use the player's `play`, `pause`, `timeupdate` and `ended` events to observe
the session. A catalog action can be bound to `ended`.

Choose speeds with a comma-separated list such as `rates="0.75,1,1.5"`.
Normal speed is always available. The default menu offers 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×,
1.5× and 2×.
